About Me

Welcome to LearnAndWrite.com

I'm Roshidullah Kyaw Naing, a passionate and multi-skilled individual currently pursuing a Bachelor of Software Engineering (Hons) at the University Malaysia of Computer Sciences and Engineering (UNIMY).

With a strong foundation in programming, web development, and database systems, I also bring real-world experience as a translator, storyteller, interviewer, and writer. My work spans blogs, articles, and humanitarian narratives—driven by a deep commitment to creativity, precision, and purpose.

I am the author of two published books:

  • "The Painful Life of Rohingya: The Voices of Rohingya Genocide Survivors to the World"
  • "The Secrets of a True Lover: The Burst of My Emotional Waves"

This platform is where I blend technology, storytelling, and lifelong learning. Here, you'll find study insights, programming resources, and powerful stories from the heart.

Join me on this journey—where we learn deeply and write fearlessly.
